# Sample interview corpus (synthetic)

**These transcripts are entirely synthetic.** They were generated to support workshop demos of LLM-assisted flexible coding (Deterding & Waters 2021; published online 2018). Any resemblance to real respondents is coincidental.

## Study design (simulated)

Topic: College planning among low-income mothers of teenagers. The instrumental/expressive typology comes from Deterding 2015, "Instrumental and Expressive Education: College Planning in the Face of Poverty" (*Sociology of Education* 88(4): 284-301), the worked example Deterding & Waters use in their Stage 2 and Stage 3 sections (pp. 729-731). That study examined low-income mothers deciding whether to return to college themselves after Hurricane Katrina; this synthetic corpus moves the typology to mothers' plans for their children, a situation the study did not examine.

The four transcripts are designed to vary along the *instrumental / expressive / mixed* typology so workshop demos can show typology assignment, construct-validity checks, negative-case hunting, and great-quote surfacing.

## Conventions

Filenames follow Deterding & Waters' p. 723 advice (`R001-Transcript.md`) so attributes can be auto-linked to documents by leading respondent ID. Speakers marked with `I:` for the (fictional) interviewer, coded `Interviewer` in the attributes and transcript headers, and the respondent's pseudonym in ALL CAPS.
