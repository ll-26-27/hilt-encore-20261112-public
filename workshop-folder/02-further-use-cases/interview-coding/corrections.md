# What was corrected, and why

Everything in this recipe was checked on November 12, 2026, against the full text of Deterding and Waters (2021), the abstract of Deterding (2015), and the publishers' pages for the other references. The originals, in the week 1 Day 4 workshop repo, are unchanged. A fuller fact-check is kept in the Learning Lab's private planning repo.

## The main corrections

1. **The corpus was described wrongly.** Every version said the synthetic interviews were "modeled on Deterding's published study of low-income mothers planning their children's college paths." That study is about mothers whose own college Hurricane Katrina interrupted. Now: they borrow her typology and move it to a new situation.
2. **The interviewer had a real person's initials.** The invented interviews listed the interviewer as "ND," Nicole Deterding's initials. Now "Interviewer."
3. **Credit.** "Mary Waters' approach" and "Waters' Stage 1" for a co-authored method whose first author is Nicole Deterding. Now Deterding and Waters, and "Stage 1 of flexible coding."
4. **Citations.** The paper is cited as 2021 (print), published online 2018. A Blee citation was wrong in three different ways in three files (the real one is an NSF workshop chapter, D&W p. 736). Katz is 1982, not 1983. One cited study ("Qiao et al. 2024") could not be found and was removed. Christou is 2023.
5. **What the paper says.** A table read backwards (most articles did describe their coding procedure); "17.3%" (the paper says nearly 20 percent); a phrase of Deterding and Waters's own credited to Glaser and Strauss; Deterding's Katrina study called "post-recession"; and the method made more absolute than its authors make it (indexing "fast," analytic coding low-agreement "and rightly so," every case required to fit, limits stated as rules).
