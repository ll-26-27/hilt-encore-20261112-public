# 04 · Make test data designed to break the tools

**Prompt:**

> Can you also generate some sample transcripts, or find some online?

**What came back:** Claude argued for generating (real transcripts are IRB-restricted or stripped of conversational texture), then wrote the four synthetic interviews, the protocol, and the attributes sheet now in [`inputs/`](../inputs/). They borrow the instrumental/expressive typology from Deterding's 2015 study, which followed low-income mothers deciding whether to return to college themselves after Hurricane Katrina, and move it to mothers' plans for their teenagers, a situation the study did not examine.

- R001 Tasha: textbook instrumental
- R002 Marisol: textbook expressive
- R003 Carla: mixed, and aware of the tension
- R004 Denise: instrumental reasoning in emotional and family language, built to trip up a naive coder

**The move worth noticing:** for a methods demo, invented data with planted edge cases shows what a tool does better than real data would.
