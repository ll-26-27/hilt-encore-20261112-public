# 09 · Commission a contextualizing essay

Not in the May 21 walkthrough. Late that morning Claude wrote a prompt for a deep research tool (which tool ran it is not recorded), and its result became the essay *From Index Cards to Indexed Codes*, now corrected in [`outputs/essay/research-result.md`](../outputs/essay/research-result.md).

The prompt asked for 2,500–3,500 words, "defensible to a senior qualitative methodologist (Mary Waters herself is in the audience)," in three parts (the tradition, what Deterding and Waters propose, where LLMs extend it), with at least 20 sources. The full prompt is kept with the original workshop repo; it is not reproduced here because three of its own statements about the paper are wrong and the essay hardened them:

- it gives "17.3%" of articles with no coding procedure as the paper's "central complaint" (the paper says nearly 20 percent, p. 719);
- it states the method's limits as rules ("not appropriate for very small samples") where the authors hedge (pp. 721, 734);
- it puts "biased, impressionistic, and anecdotal" in quotation marks without a source; the essay then credited the phrase to Glaser and Strauss. It is Deterding and Waters's own (p. 711).

**The move worth noticing:** the essay is the one product nobody checked in May, and the one that left the building, without an author. The fix is the method's own: Stage 3 looks for the cases that don't fit.
