# Interview coding: a methods paper becomes a working tool

This example follows Deterding and Waters’s flexible-coding method. Four synthetic interviews go through protocol-based indexing and two negative-case exercises; a methods paragraph states what happened and what did not. The corpus adapts Deterding’s instrumental/expressive typology to mothers planning their children’s education; the underlying study concerns mothers’ own return to college after Hurricane Katrina.

Start with the [recipe card](README.md), then the [run instructions](operations/10-run-the-recipe.md). The ten [operations](operations/) document the original build, from reading the paper through auditing the skills and running the demonstration.

The saved example performs Stage 1 indexing and Stage 3 negative-case exercises. Stage 2 analytic coding, a codebook, a second-coder pass and inter-coder reliability were not performed. The exercises do not establish a typology from this corpus.

The November 12 corrections cover attribution, corpus framing, fictional interviewer metadata, references and the essay. Read [corrections.md](corrections.md) and [PORT.md](PORT.md). The corrected [essay](outputs/essay/research-result.md) is AI-assisted workshop material, not an article reviewed by the source paper’s authors.
