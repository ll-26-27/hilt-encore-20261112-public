# 3. Check and explain

Compare at least one readout with a hand calculation. Test slider endpoints, units, reset/play controls where present, and limiting behaviour. Check whether doubling time, radius, mass or power has the effect predicted by the chosen equation. For coffee, distinguish extraction fraction from beverage strength; for tahini, inspect the non-monotonic water response.

Compare with the kitchen target in the brief and record discrepancies. Have another person drive the simulation and explain the rule they see. Record remaining scientific and accessibility questions; a plausible animation is not evidence that the model is correct.
