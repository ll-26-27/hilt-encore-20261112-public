Make one week's practice explicitly AI-free, focused on the physical capture end of the work, so later AI use has something real to work on.

## How it runs

Students choose one or two challenges for the week: recreate a look they admire, build a home recording corner, walk and talk to camera, borrow equipment and use it. "No AI this week; this is about the capture end of the chain."

## With Claude

None.

## Why it works

It establishes that the human-made material comes first, and gives students a baseline of their own unassisted work to compare with later.

## Time and materials

The usual weekly practice time. Borrowed equipment.

## Variations

A no-AI draft before every AI-assisted revision; one no-AI problem set per unit.
