Do one editing task three ways (a free local tool, a paid AI model, a command-line tool) and compare quality, cost, time, and control.

## How it runs

Students remove the background from the same photo with a local model, an AI image model, and ImageMagick, then lay the three results side by side and choose one for a stated purpose.

## With Claude

Ask Claude Code for a small page with one photo and three buttons, one per method, each writing a new file beside the original, or run the three commands in Claude Code directly.

## Why it works

Students learn that AI is one option among tools, with trade-offs they can name.

## Time and materials

30 minutes; a local install of the free tools; a few cents for the model.

## Variations

Noise reduction three ways; transcription three ways; translation three ways.
