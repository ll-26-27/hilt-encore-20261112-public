Give students a tool that is hard to control on purpose, so that wrestling with it produces happy accidents and a feel for the medium.

## How it runs

Hand out a tool with a tiny margin for error (a lens with a few millimeters of focus; a Lensbaby; a dull carving tool; a fountain pen). Students make a series with it, then show their best accident and say what the resistance taught them.

## With Claude

None during the exercise. Afterward, students may ask an image model to imitate their accident and compare.

## Why it works

Difficulty makes the medium visible, and the accidents students keep become choices they can explain.

## Time and materials

20 minutes. One difficult tool per group.

## Variations

Write with a constraint (no letter e); compose on a limited instrument; code without autocomplete for one task.
