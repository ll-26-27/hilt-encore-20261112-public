A webcam and a hand-tracking model let a student's fingertip, raised hand, or step into a zone of the frame fire a sound or an image.

## How it runs

Draw two or three zones on the camera view. Students move into them and hear what fires; then they choreograph a ten-second piece where every cue comes from their body, with no buttons.

## With Claude

Ask Claude Code: "Use MediaPipe's hand and pose landmarkers in the browser with the webcam; draw these zones on a mirrored preview; when a fingertip enters a zone, fire its cue once, with a short re-arm delay." Models load from a CDN; no key.

## Why it works

Students experience the move from operating a machine to performing with one, and see the gap between what the camera sees and what they meant.

## Time and materials

30 minutes; a webcam, good light, a projector; an afternoon to build.

## Variations

Dance (zones as places on a floor); physical therapy exercises with feedback; a museum installation where visitors trigger narration.
