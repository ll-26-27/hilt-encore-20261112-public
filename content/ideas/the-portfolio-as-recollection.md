End a course by having students gather everything they made and said over the term and turn it into an account, with AI helping to find what's there.

## How it runs

Students collect their work, drafts, reflections, and transcripts in one folder. They use AI to index and search it, then write and record a final reflection that presents the work, explains the process, and makes the case for what they built. Revisions are referenced so the reader sees what changed.

## With Claude

In Claude Code, in the portfolio folder: "Index everything here by date and kind in `index.md`; list the five places where my approach changed, with the file that shows each." The student verifies each item against the file and builds the reflection from what they find.

## Why it works

Recollecting a term's work in one place shows students their own development, and the oral reflection asks them to stand behind it: "the version of each piece that counts is the version you stand behind at the end of the term."

## Time and materials

A week outside class. Claude Code.

## Variations

A mid-term recollection; a group portfolio; a recollection interview with a peer.
