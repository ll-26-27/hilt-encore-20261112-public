Schedule the interview or presentation after the piece is made and edited, so students account for decisions rather than narrate plans.

## How it runs

Order a project's steps as make → edit → deliver → talk. The oral part comes last and asks about choices visible in the finished work. Earlier check-ins can still happen, but they are framed as working sessions, not the assessed account.

## With Claude

None needed for the ordering; pair with "A question skill that reads the work" for the final conversation.

## Why it works

Before the work exists, students describe what they hope to do; after, they have to explain what they did, which is where the learning shows.

## Time and materials

A change to the calendar; no extra time.

## Variations

Keep one short "pitch" conversation early, clearly ungraded.
