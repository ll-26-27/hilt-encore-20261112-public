Open a tools session with two quick prompts that fail in different ways: one gets a fact wrong, the other gives a narrow default.

## How it runs

Everyone asks a fast model to multiply two large numbers "immediately, without thinking," then asks it to solve the same problem with code. Then everyone asks two image tools for "a happy couple" and posts the results to one channel. The class names what the wrong answer and the images have in common.

## With Claude

In Claude Desktop: "Multiply 48,917 × 36,284. Answer immediately." Then: "Now solve it by writing and running code." In Claude Code, a student can build a table across five models with a correct-answer column: "Write a script that asks each model in `models.json` this question five times and shows whether each answer matches the true product."

## Why it works

It separates two failure modes students will meet all term (fabricated facts; flattened defaults) and shows a fix for the first: make the model compute rather than guess.

## Time and materials

15 minutes. Chat accounts; optionally an API key.

## Variations

Dates of a historical event; a citation; "draw a doctor"; a chemistry calculation.
