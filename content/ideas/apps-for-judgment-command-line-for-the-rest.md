Teach one standard app per medium for the decisions, then hand the repeatable work around those decisions to the AI at the command line.

## How it runs

Tour one app per medium (an audio editor, a video editor, a photo editor): import, the basic edit, export, the shortcuts to learn first. Then show the same repeatable jobs (normalize audio, compress footage, remove a background) done by asking the AI to run command-line tools. Students decide which steps they want to keep in their own hands.

## With Claude

In Claude Code: "Normalize the audio in `interview.wav` to −16 LUFS," "Compress `raw.mov` for the web," "Key out the green screen in `take.mp4`." Save each working command as a recipe with a "what to ask Claude" line, and turn the ones you reuse into skills.

## Why it works

"The editing apps put every decision behind a button. The command-line tools put every decision in a line of text." Students learn where judgment lives and where automation fits.

## Time and materials

90 minutes. Apps installed; Claude Code; ffmpeg.

## Variations

A spreadsheet for analysis decisions plus scripted data cleaning; a word processor for drafting plus scripted formatting.
