# Teaching and learning ideas

## Start here

- [The five-minute unscripted reflection video](the-five-minute-unscripted-reflection-video.md) (mixed): After each class, every student records at least five minutes of themselves talking, unscripted and unedited, about what they did, what stuck, and the choices they made.
- [The stealth oral exam: podcast interviews prepared from each other's context folders](the-stealth-oral-exam-podcast-interviews-prepared-from-each-others-con.md) (mixed): Students interview each other about their finished projects as a podcast, each host preparing by having Claude read the guest's context folder, so the conversation works as an oral exam that doesn't feel like one.
- [Build your own interview machine to practice speaking](build-your-own-interview-machine-to-practice-speaking.md) (build-with-claude-code): Students build, with Claude Code, a small app that records a two-person interview, transcribes it as they talk, and suggests follow-up questions, then use it to practice interviewing and being interviewed.
- [The AI as interviewer: it writes only when you say "write it"](the-ai-as-interviewer-it-writes-only-when-you-say-write-it.md) (perform-in-claude): Flip the usual prompt: the model asks the questions, one at a time, and writes nothing until the student says so.
- [Your origin story as a comic](your-origin-story-as-a-comic.md) (perform-in-claude): an interview or self-portrait becomes a character sheet and a four- or eight-panel comic, and the student says where it got them wrong.
- [Your "I Want" song](your-i-want-song.md) (perform-in-claude): the AI interviews you first and writes only when you say "write it", then a song in a genre, then the lines that could be anyone's.
- [Let AI misrepresent you](let-ai-misrepresent-you.md) (perform-in-claude): Have AI portray each student (in a story, an image, a song) from their own words, and ask them to name exactly where it gets them wrong.
- [Required context](required-context.md) (perform-in-claude): Beside required and optional reading, add a third list: texts students are not expected to read cover to cover but must load into their AI tools while they work.
- [An AI producer that only speaks when asked](an-ai-producer-that-only-speaks-when-asked.md) (mixed): During a live interview, a third person (the producer) can highlight any passage of the running transcript and ask an AI for two follow-up questions about it; the AI never interrupts on its own.
- [Framed and unframed questions side by side](framed-and-unframed-questions-side-by-side.md) (perform-in-claude): Ask an AI the same question twice, once with all the context and instructions and once with only the passage itself, and compare the two answers.
- [Studies for an agent](studies-for-an-agent.md) (perform-in-claude): Before writing instructions for an AI to do a task, do the task with the AI by hand three times and write down what you corrected.
- [A theater of agents as a folder of Markdown](a-theater-of-agents-as-a-folder-of-markdown.md) (build-with-claude-code): Students design a system of AI agents as a folder of plain text files (a premise, a cast of characters with voices and secrets, a judge or a director), run it, and watch what the system does.
- [Plant a file the AI can't read](plant-a-file-the-ai-cant-read.md) (build-with-claude-code): When testing any AI pipeline, include one item it can't possibly handle and check that it says so instead of pretending.
- [One context document, many forms](one-context-document-many-forms.md) (build-with-claude-code): Build one rich document of source material, then run it through several prompts and several models, and compare what each makes of it.
- [Practice with a floor and a ceiling](practice-with-a-floor-and-a-ceiling.md) (mixed): Assign some work by time, not by product: a set number of minutes of practice, after which you are done, so students practice regularly instead of cramming.
- [The told-afterward report](the-told-afterward-report.md) (perform-in-claude): After each class, the instructor records a few minutes of what happened, and an AI turns it into a dated report beside the plan, so plan and outcome can be compared.
- [The process record is the turn-in](the-process-record-is-the-turn-in.md) (perform-in-claude): When students work in a repository with an AI, have them turn in the history (commits and the AI session log) along with the work, instead of condensing their process into a separate write-up.
