Before making a remix or a project, students write down what they are taking from, what they intend to do with it, and how they'll know it worked; the work is judged partly against that declaration.

## How it runs

A one-paragraph declaration at the start: sources and references, the intended move, the criterion of success. After making, students reread it and say where they hit, missed, or changed course.

## With Claude

Students ask Claude to critique the declaration for vagueness before they start ("What in this plan is too vague to judge afterwards?"), and later to compare the finished piece with the declaration.

## Why it works

It makes intention visible and gradable, and separates a deliberate choice from an AI default.

## Time and materials

Ten minutes at the start, ten at the end.

## Variations

A declared-direction card for a long project; declarations shared with a partner who holds you to them.
