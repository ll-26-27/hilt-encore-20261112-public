Every submitted piece ends with one short paragraph: what the AI generated, what the student changed, and why.

## How it runs

Add the requirement to the assignment: three sentences at the end, in the student's words. Read them aloud in a few critiques; use them as prompts in oral checks.

## With Claude

Students can ask Claude to list, from the session history, every generated element in the final piece; they write the colophon themselves from that list.

## Why it works

It operationalizes "appropriately acknowledged" AI use in a form short enough to always do, and it makes students notice their own contributions.

## Time and materials

Five minutes per piece.

## Variations

A visual colophon (a diagram of which parts came from where); a spoken colophon at the start of a presentation.
