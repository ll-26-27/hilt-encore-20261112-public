Teach students the names of a few powerful tools and what each does, and let the AI handle the exact commands.

## How it runs

Keep a short list of tool names on the board (e.g. a video downloader, a media converter, an image converter, a transcriber). For each, do one live task by asking the AI to use it. Students add one tool name per week to their own list.

## With Claude

In Claude Code: "Use ffmpeg to cut seconds 12–20 of `clip.mp4` into a GIF," "Use yt-dlp to download this clip," "Use ImageMagick to make a contact sheet of these photos." Claude writes and runs the command; the student keeps the line in a `recipes.md`.

## Why it works

Remembering what is possible, and its name, is the scarce skill; syntax can be looked up. Students build a vocabulary of capabilities.

## Time and materials

15 minutes. Claude Code; tools installed with a package manager.

## Variations

Statistics libraries in a methods course; database tools; citation managers.
