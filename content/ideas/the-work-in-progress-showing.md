In the last weeks, each student gives a five-to-eight-minute account of where their project stands and where it's going, followed by response.

## How it runs

Projects run or screen; the student explains what works, what doesn't, and the next steps, unscripted; peers and the instructor respond with one question each. Use it to brief the final portfolio and reflection.

## With Claude

Before presenting, the student asks Claude to read their project folder and "ask me the three questions I'd least like to be asked about this," then practices answering aloud.

## Why it works

Explaining unfinished work out loud forces students to say what they intend, and response arrives while there's still time to act on it.

## Time and materials

10 minutes per student. A projector.

## Variations

Poster-style in parallel; recorded for those who can't attend; paired with a written one-pager.
