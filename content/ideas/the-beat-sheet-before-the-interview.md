After a short pre-interview, an AI turns the notes and transcript into a one-page brief for the interviewer: the guest, the one thing, four to six beats, the best questions, names to say right, what to handle with care, and claims to check.

## How it runs

A five-minute pre-interview, recorded and transcribed. The interviewer asks for the beat sheet, reads it, crosses out what's wrong, and uses the corrected page for the real interview the next day.

## With Claude

Paste the transcript and notes into Claude Desktop with: "Write the producer's beat sheet for the host: one page in Markdown, from the notes and the transcript only, with exactly these sections: The guest; The one thing; Beats; Questions; Names and terms; Handle with care; Check before air." Or add a Beat sheet button to an interview tool.

## Why it works

The "check before air" section trains verification: in one run, the beat sheet caught a garbled transcription and said to confirm the wording before quoting it. Correcting the page is where the interviewer learns the material.

## Time and materials

Five minutes to generate; fifteen to correct; a transcript.

## Variations

Before office hours (brief yourself on a student's draft); before a guest speaker visit; before a site visit or a client meeting.
