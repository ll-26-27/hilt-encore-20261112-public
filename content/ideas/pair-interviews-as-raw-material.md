Before any AI work, students interview each other for five minutes each way on their phones, and the recording becomes the source the AI works from.

## How it runs

Pairs take turns: five minutes asking, five answering, recorded on a phone. Each student transcribes their own recording (or takes 8–12 lines of notes), and that transcript is the only material they give the model.

## With Claude

Drop the audio file into a folder and ask Claude Code: "Transcribe `interview.m4a` to `interview.md` with timestamps, then list the three most specific details I said." Then use the transcript in the next activity.

## Why it works

"The conversation is the context; without it, every model writes 'follow my dreams.'" A human conversation produces specific material a cold prompt never will.

## Time and materials

15 minutes. Phones; a transcription tool.

## Variations

Interview a family member about a historical event; interview a lab partner about their method before writing it up.
