Small groups map and compare the AI products in the news, then present their findings, building their own small website as the visual aid if they like.

## How it runs

Each group takes two or three products (big-lab assistants, open-source agent frameworks) and reads their launch pages and demos closely: what they promise, how they look, what they call themselves. Groups map them against each other and report out in five minutes.

## With Claude

Groups can ask Claude Code for "a one-page site comparing these three products in a table, with their own words quoted and linked," and present from it.

## Why it works

Close reading marketing as a text builds critical distance, and when students build their own visual aids the presentation becomes a small act of making. Students may build presentation sites without being asked; that is worth noticing aloud.

## Time and materials

40 minutes. Laptops; Claude Code optional.

## Variations

Close-read the ed-tech products sold to your school; compare three news outlets' AI coverage.
