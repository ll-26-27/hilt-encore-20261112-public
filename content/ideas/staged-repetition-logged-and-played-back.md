Have students repeat a small skill many times, log each attempt, and later see or hear their attempts played back in sequence.

## How it runs

Choose a skill with a visible result (twenty photos with a difficult lens, ten takes of a spoken intro, fifty quick sketches). Students do the repetitions and save every attempt with a timestamp. The instructor collects them and, a week later, shows a sequence back to each student (or the class) and asks what changed.

## With Claude

Build with Claude Code a small gallery that reads a folder of attempts and shows them in time order with filenames and dates (ask for "a static HTML page that reads every image in this folder, sorts by capture time, and shows them as a contact sheet with the time under each"); for audio, a page that plays the takes in order.

## Why it works

Repetition builds skill, and seeing your own progression makes the learning visible and memorable.

## Time and materials

Thirty minutes of practice; a shared folder; a few minutes to build the gallery.

## Variations

Repetitions of a lab technique on video; handwriting samples; a pronunciation drill in a language course.
