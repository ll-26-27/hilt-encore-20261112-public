After learning something new with AI's help, go for a walk and explain it aloud into your phone without notes, as a self-administered closed-book exam.

## How it runs

At the end of a study or build session, the learner walks (ten to twenty minutes) and talks through what they learned: the steps, the reasons, what went wrong. Afterwards they transcribe it and compare it with the session's notes, marking what they forgot or got wrong.

## With Claude

Drop the recording into a Claude Code project with the session's notes and ask: "Compare this transcript with these notes. What did I explain correctly, what did I get wrong, and what did I leave out?" Save the comparison next to the notes.

## Why it works

AI lets people encounter more than they can internalize; retrieval without notes is how it sticks, and speaking it aloud shows the gaps.

## Time and materials

Fifteen to twenty minutes; a phone.

## Variations

Assign it to students as the weekly reflection; do it in pairs on a walk; do it daily during an intensive workshop.
