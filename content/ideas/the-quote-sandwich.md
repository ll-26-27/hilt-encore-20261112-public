When an AI quotes a source to you, trust only the verbatim quotation in the middle and treat what comes before and after it as the AI's framing to check.

## How it runs

Teach students to ask AI for answers that include a short verbatim quotation from the source, clearly marked. In discussion or an oral exam, students close-read the quotation itself and compare it with the AI's claims around it. Spot-check a few quotations against the original in class.

## With Claude

In Claude Code with the source texts in the folder, ask: "Answer with at least one verbatim quotation of one to three sentences from these files, in quotation marks, with the file and line; put your interpretation before and after it." Then ask Claude to verify each quotation by searching the file (it can grep), and show the result.

## Why it works

It gives students a concrete way to use AI with texts without outsourcing the reading, and makes the gap between source and summary visible.

## Time and materials

Fifteen minutes to introduce; the texts as files.

## Variations

Students build the verification step themselves as a small script; use primary sources in history or legal texts in law.
