Under an overhead camera, students draw a rectangle on paper with a marker, and a projector plays a video clip inside it.

## How it runs

Set a camera and projector over a table. Students draw boxes on paper; the system finds each rectangle and maps a clip into it. They rearrange the paper to compose a moving collage.

## With Claude

Ask Claude Code for a page that finds dark rectangles in the camera feed with OpenCV.js, computes the perspective transform, and draws a chosen video into each one on a projector window. Expect an afternoon and a physical test of the lighting.

## Why it works

Paper and projection make digital media tangible, and the failures (glare, a broken line) teach how computer vision sees.

## Time and materials

An overhead camera, a projector, paper, thick dark markers; a large download for OpenCV.js.

## Variations

Projection mapping onto models in architecture; a storyboard table where each panel plays its shot.
