Students photograph real textures (scratches, paper, film grain, walls) and layer them over AI-generated images to break the generated polish, then explain each choice.

## How it runs

Twenty minutes collecting textures with phones around the building. Then, in an image editor or with a script, layer textures over a generated image using blend modes. Students compare before and after and say what the texture changed.

## With Claude

Ask Claude Code for "a script that takes an image and a folder of texture photos, and writes ten versions blending one texture each with multiply and overlay modes at three opacities, plus an HTML contact sheet."

## Why it works

It turns the critique of generic AI images into a making exercise, and brings the physical world back into the pipeline.

## Time and materials

Forty-five minutes; phones; an image editor or Claude Code.

## Variations

Audio: layer recorded room tone under synthetic speech; text: rewrite a generated paragraph with words from a field notebook.
