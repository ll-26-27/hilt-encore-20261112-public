Start every session by projecting a selection of what students posted since last time, for ten minutes, before anything new.

## How it runs

Students post work, links, and reflections to a shared channel during the week. Before class, the instructor (or an assistant, or a student) picks a handful. Class opens with them on the wall; each poster gets thirty seconds; the instructor connects them to the day's plan.

## With Claude

Build with Claude Code a page that pulls the week's posts from an export of the channel and shows them as a slideshow (ask for "a page that reads this JSON export of channel messages and files from the last seven days and shows each as a full-screen slide with the poster's first name only"). Keep it inside the class.

## Why it works

Students keep posting when they know it will be seen, and the class begins with their work rather than the instructor's.

## Time and materials

Ten minutes of class; a projector; a shared channel.

## Variations

Students curate in rotation; a themed wall (best failure, best question).
