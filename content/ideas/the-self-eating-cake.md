A discussion prompt: which parts of your work would you hand to an AI the way you'd hand driving to a self-driving car, and which are the cake you want to eat yourself?

## How it runs

Pose the image: you might want a self-driving car, but nobody wants a self-eating cake. Students sort the parts of a current assignment into "car" and "cake" and explain. Discuss what happens when students treat their coursework as all car.

## With Claude

None needed; optionally ask Claude to argue for automating each "cake" item and have students rebut.

## Why it works

It reframes the AI question from cheating to what the student wants to experience and learn, which is a conversation students engage with.

## Time and materials

Ten to fifteen minutes.
