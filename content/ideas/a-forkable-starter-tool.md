Give students the smallest working version of a tool they'll need, and invite them to fork and change it rather than use a finished product.

## How it runs

Build a minimal tool (a clip trimmer: mark segments, export each as a clip). Students run it on their own material, then each adds one feature they want, using the AI.

## With Claude

Ask Claude Code: "Build a local page where I load a video, mark many in and out points with I and O, and export each segment as its own clip with ffmpeg on my machine." Students then: "Add a field to name each clip," or "add a keyboard shortcut to jump back two seconds."

## Why it works

A tool students can change becomes something they understand, and each change is a small, safe first piece of software.

## Time and materials

An hour to build the starter; 30 minutes in class. Claude Code; ffmpeg.

## Variations

A starter flashcard app, a starter data viewer, a starter annotation tool.
