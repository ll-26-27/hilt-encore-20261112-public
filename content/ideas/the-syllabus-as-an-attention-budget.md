Plan a course's reading and practice in hours of attention rather than pages, and say so in the syllabus.

## How it runs

For each week, list the activities and estimate the hours each should take: reading, loading context and asking, making, reflecting. Cap the total (for example, one hour of required outside work plus open-ended project time). Share the budget with students and ask them, mid-term, where their hours went.

## With Claude

Give Claude Code the syllabus and ask for a week-by-week table of estimated hours by activity, flagged where a week exceeds the cap; revise together.

## Why it works

Page counts no longer describe the work when AI can read for students; hours describe what you are asking of their attention, and make the trade-offs visible.

## Time and materials

An hour of planning.

## Variations

Students log their own hours and compare with the budget; the budget includes a floor (minimum time) for practices.
