A discussion move: for people, repeating something is how you learn it; for AI, something repeated is a sign it can be automated. Ask students which of their repeated tasks belong in which category.

## How it runs

Students list three things they do repeatedly in their studies or work. For each, they say whether repeating it builds a skill they want to own, or whether it is a candidate for automation, and what they would lose by automating it. Discuss in pairs, then as a class.

## With Claude

Ask Claude to propose how it would automate each task, then evaluate what the student would stop learning if it did.

## Why it works

It gives students a principled way to decide what to hand off to AI, framed around their own learning.

## Time and materials

Fifteen minutes.

## Variations

Apply it to a profession students want to enter.
