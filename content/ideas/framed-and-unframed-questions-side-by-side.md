Ask an AI the same question twice, once with all the context and instructions and once with only the passage itself, and compare the two answers.

## How it runs

Whenever students ask an AI for a follow-up question, critique, or summary, they send a second request alongside it with no system prompt, no notes, and no background: only the passage and a one-line ask. They read both and say which is better and why.

## With Claude

In Claude Desktop, open two chats: one with your notes and instructions, one empty, and paste the same passage and ask into each. In a tool, ask Claude Code: "For every Question request, also send an unframed one: system prompt 'You know nothing about the show, the speakers, or any project', the few transcript lines around the passage, and the note. Tag it Unframed."

## Why it works

Students see what context does to a model's output, and sometimes that the unframed answer is fresher; both lessons teach them to write context on purpose.

## Time and materials

Ten minutes of discussion; no extra cost beyond a second request.

## Variations

Feedback on essays (with the rubric vs without); research questions (with the reading list vs without); image prompts with and without a style guide.
