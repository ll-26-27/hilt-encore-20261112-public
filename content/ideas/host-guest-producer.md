Run any interview with three rotating roles so the person asking questions can stay present while someone else watches the screen.

## How it runs

The producer does a short pre-interview with the guest and keeps notes; during the live interview the producer holds the laptop, generates or selects follow-up questions, and passes the good ones to the host on a card or in a whisper; the host never looks at a screen. Rotate roles every fifteen minutes.

## With Claude

The producer runs a live transcript (a small web app built with Claude Code that records two microphones and transcribes each speaker) and highlights a passage to ask Claude for one follow-up question. Ask Claude Code for "a Next.js page that records from an audio interface with two inputs, splits speech at pauses, transcribes each utterance, and lets me highlight a line to get one follow-up question."

## Why it works

Separating listening from question-generation lets the host practice attention and follow-up, and makes the AI a backstage helper rather than the interviewer.

## Time and materials

Fifteen minutes per round; two microphones and an interface, or two phones; a laptop.

## Variations

Producer as a human only (no AI); add a fourth role, the editor, who marks moments for a highlight reel.
