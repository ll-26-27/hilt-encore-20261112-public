Ask the AI to preserve each idea as a separate, short note before it synthesizes, because synthesis adds structure that wasn't in your thinking.

## How it runs

After a brainstorm or a dictation, have the AI split the material into one-idea notes, each with its date and source, and no merging. Only later, and on request, ask it to group or relate them, keeping the atomic notes intact.

## With Claude

"Split this transcript into one Markdown file per idea in ideas/, each with the date, a one-line title, and the verbatim passage; do not combine or summarize across ideas."

## Why it works

Ideas stay findable and attributable, and the learner (or instructor) does the relating, which is where the thinking is.

## Time and materials

A few minutes per session.

## Variations

Students use it for research notes (a Zettelkasten built with AI).
