Every page of a course site has a light print version, so any tool's output (a trace, a transcript, a list) can go on paper for marking up.

## How it runs

Students print the artifact they want to discuss, mark it up by hand in groups, and photograph the marked pages back into their notebook.

## With Claude

Ask Claude Code for a `/print` route that renders any page, a folder, or a named set on Letter with a light stylesheet.

## Why it works

Paper slows reading down and makes collaborative annotation easy; it's a counterweight to screens.

## Time and materials

An hour to build once.

## Variations

Print sets for a seminar; a printed reader of AI-generated essays to critique.
