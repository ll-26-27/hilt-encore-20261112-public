Build a collection of fact-checked lists about a field (where our hours go, who gets paid, the most popular things in each medium), each with what it counts, as of when, and how sure the list is.

## How it runs

Each student researches one list with Claude, records its sources and date, and rates its confidence. The class reads across the lists and argues about what they show.

## With Claude

Ask Claude to research and draft one list as Markdown with frontmatter (what it counts, as of, confidence, sources), then verify every number against the source yourself. Ask Claude Code for a site that renders a folder of lists as groups, with embedded players for any linked media.

## Why it works

Numbers get context (as of when, how sure), and students practice checking AI research instead of trusting it.

## Time and materials

An afternoon of research per list; an hour to build the site.

## Variations

Public health statistics; the history of an art form in ten lists; a field's "most cited" lists.
