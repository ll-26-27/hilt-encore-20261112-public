Run two speech recognizers (or two transcription models) on the same voice at the same time and compare what each heard.

## How it runs

A student reads a paragraph with names and technical terms. Both transcripts appear side by side; the class marks the differences and guesses why (a hint list, a different model, latency).

## With Claude

Ask Claude Code for a page that sends the same microphone to the browser's speech recognition and to a cloud model, with each one's text and latency in its own column.

## Why it works

Students stop treating a transcript as the truth and learn to choose and check a tool.

## Time and materials

15 minutes; a microphone; one key.

## Variations

Two translation engines; two OCR engines on the same handwriting.
