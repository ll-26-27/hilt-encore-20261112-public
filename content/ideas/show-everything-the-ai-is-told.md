Every tool that calls a model shows, on the page and before anything is sent, the full system prompt, the context, and the request, and lets the user edit them.

## How it runs

Students open the "what gets sent" panel before they press Record or Run, read what the model will be told about them and their work, and change it if they want. Afterward, they explain one change they made and what it did.

## With Claude

Ask Claude Code to keep every prompt in one file that both the page and the server import, render it in a panel with editable fields, and show the exact request body before sending.

## Why it works

Students learn that an AI's behavior comes from instructions people wrote, and they practice writing them; it also makes consent concrete.

## Time and materials

Part of building any tool.

## Variations

A course chatbot whose system prompt is public and open to amendment by the class.
