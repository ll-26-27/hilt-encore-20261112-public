Add agents (or students) whose only job is to watch a multi-agent run from outside and write notes on what is happening: a chorus, a critic, a referee.

## How it runs

Run a cast of agents with two observers that never speak in the scene: one comments like a chorus, one rates the run. Students act as human observers at the same time and compare their notes with the agents'.

## With Claude

In an agent theater, an `observers/` folder with one Markdown file per observer, read every few rounds; or in Claude Code: "After every round, give the transcript to an observer prompted as a chorus and save its note."

## Why it works

Observing is a skill (what is this system doing?), and comparing human and AI observations teaches both.

## Time and materials

30 minutes; a cent or two per run.

## Variations

A debate with an AI fact-checker observer; a design crit with an observer that tracks which criteria came up.
