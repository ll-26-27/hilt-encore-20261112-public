Students photograph handwritten pages and get a faithful transcription, with the photos embedded below it, saved as a note.

## How it runs

After a paper exercise (a sketch, a map, notes), students photograph their pages, upload them in order, and get a Markdown note that preserves their wording and marks anything illegible. They check it against the photos.

## With Claude

Ask Claude Code for an upload page that sends up to 12 images to a vision model with "Transcribe faithfully; preserve the wording; mark illegible text as [illegible]" and writes the note with the images embedded. In Claude Desktop: attach the photos and ask for the same.

## Why it works

Paper thinking enters the same searchable record as typed and spoken notes, so students don't have to choose between the pen and the archive.

## Time and materials

About an hour to build; a phone or webcam.

## Variations

Whiteboard photos after group work; lab notebook pages; marked-up drafts.
