Give an AI helper a narrow, kind job with a firm constraint, like offering up to three optional opening jokes where the butt is the host or the situation, never the guest, and "no useful joke here" is a fine answer.

## How it runs

Students write the constraint into the sidekick's prompt, run it on several items, and check every output against the rule. Then they write their own narrow-job agent with one constraint and test it.

## With Claude

A short prompt file for the role, used in Claude Desktop or a bot: the job, the limit (zero to three), the rule about the butt, and permission to say nothing.

## Why it works

Students learn that good agents have small jobs and explicit limits, including permission to do nothing.

## Time and materials

20 minutes.

## Variations

A devil's advocate who must cite the text; an encourager who must name something specific.
