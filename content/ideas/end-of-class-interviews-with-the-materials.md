Close a session by having students interviewed, one at a time or in pairs, about what they just did, with the session's readings loaded into an AI that helps connect the experience to ideas after the fact.

## How it runs

In the last fifteen minutes, pairs take turns: one interviews the other about the activity, with an AI that has the readings, suggesting one connection to a reading per answer. The interviewee accepts or rejects each connection and says why.

## With Claude

In Claude Desktop, a Project with the readings: "I'll paste what a student said about today's activity. Suggest one connection to a specific passage in these readings, quoted, and one question that tests whether the connection holds."

## Why it works

The link between doing and theory can be made right after the experience, even by students who haven't read everything, and evaluating the AI's suggested connection is itself the reading.

## Time and materials

Fifteen minutes; Claude Desktop; the readings as files.

## Variations

Do it as a written exit ticket; record the interviews for the reflection.
