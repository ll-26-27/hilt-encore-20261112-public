Early in a project, each student has a short recorded one-on-one with the instructor, prepared by an AI-written briefing on the student's work so far.

## How it runs

Before the meeting, an AI reads what the student has posted or made and writes a one-page briefing with questions. In the meeting (fifteen to thirty minutes), talk through what they tried, what surprised them, and where they want to go. Record it (with permission); it is the student's first time being interviewed about their work.

## With Claude

In Claude Code with the student's folder: "Write a one-page briefing on this student's work so far: what they made, what seems to interest them, and five open questions about where the project could go." The instructor edits it before the meeting.

## Why it works

It sets a direction together, models the recorded interview as a form before students are graded on one, and the briefing helps the instructor listen rather than catch up.

## Time and materials

Fifteen to thirty minutes per student; a recorder.

## Variations

Pairs of students instead of instructor meetings; a TA runs them.
