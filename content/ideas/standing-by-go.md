Run a short performance or presentation from a numbered cue list, with one person calling cues and another pressing Go, the way a theater runs a show.

## How it runs

Groups write a cue list of eight to ten cues for a two-minute piece. One student is the stage manager (calls "standing by … go"), one the operator (presses Go), one or two perform. Run it twice; the second time, the operator never looks at the performer.

## With Claude

Ask Claude Code for a cue list page that reads `cue-list.json`, highlights the cue standing by, fires it on Go (Space), moves back and skip with the arrows, and blacks out on Esc. Students write the JSON or ask Claude to write it from their script.

## Why it works

It teaches the discipline of timing, roles, and calling, and shows how a whole show compresses to one button when the preparation is in the list.

## Time and materials

40 minutes; a projector; the cue list tool.

## Variations

A lab demonstration with numbered steps; a group presentation where each member runs a section; a radio play.
