Have students make one long, substantial piece, then cut it into short pieces for different platforms, and compare what each version keeps and loses.

## How it runs

After finishing a long piece (a video essay, a podcast, a paper), students produce three short versions for different audiences or platforms. In critique, they explain what they chose to keep in each and why.

## With Claude

With the transcript in Claude Code: "Find five moments under sixty seconds that stand on their own, with timestamps"; then "write an ffmpeg command for each clip." Students choose and edit; Claude does the cutting.

## Why it works

It separates making from distribution, and teaches that every format is a set of choices about the same material.

## Time and materials

One session after a long project; editing tools.

## Variations

A research paper into an abstract, a poster, a thread; one project, four platforms.
