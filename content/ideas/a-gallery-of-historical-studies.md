Show students historical sketches, studies, and rehearsal records in their own field, so that a study (an attempt made to get better, not to finish) becomes a form they can make.

## How it runs

Assemble 20–50 examples: painters' studies, rehearsal promptbooks, storyboards, a writer's exercises. Students pick one, say what was being practiced, and then make a study of their own in the same spirit.

## With Claude

In Claude Code: "Build a gallery from `studies/*.md` (each with image URL, maker, date, what it practiced, source), filterable by field, with a page for each." Have Claude draft entries, then check each against a museum or archive source.

## Why it works

Seeing masters practice makes practice respectable and gives students models for what a study looks like.

## Time and materials

Prep a few hours; 20 minutes in class. Claude Code.

## Variations

Drafts of famous papers; early versions of code projects; rehearsal footage.
