Start any lesson on live media with a grid of buttons, each one a cue (a sound, an image, a color, words, a blackout), that plays on a second window on the projector.

## How it runs

The instructor shows the pad: press 1, the title appears; 2, a ping; 9, blackout. Students then edit one small file that lists the buttons to make the pad their own and perform a 30-second sequence for the room.

## With Claude

Ask Claude Code: "Make a stage page (black, full screen) that listens on a BroadcastChannel and plays cues: sound, video, image, text, color, blackout, stop, with fades. Make a launch page with nine buttons from buttons.js, keys 1–9, Space for the next one, Esc for blackout." Students change `buttons.js` or ask Claude to.

## Why it works

It strips installation and show control down to one idea students can hold (an input fires a cue on a stage), which later inputs (voice, MIDI, a hand) only replace.

## Time and materials

20 minutes; a laptop, a projector, speakers; an hour to build.

## Variations

A soundboard for a radio drama; a cue pad for a presentation; a science demo where each button shows the next stage of a process.
