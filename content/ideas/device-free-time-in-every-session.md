Set aside part of every class for work with no screens: markers, pens, ink, paper, talk.

## How it runs

Ten to twenty minutes each session, devices away: sketch, write by hand, plan on paper, talk in pairs. Then photograph the results and bring them into the digital work.

## With Claude

Afterward, students photograph their pages and ask Claude to transcribe them into their notes folder, correcting the transcription themselves.

## Why it works

It protects attention, brings the body and hand into the work, and gives the AI-assisted parts of the course something human to start from.

## Time and materials

Ten to twenty minutes; paper and markers.

## Variations

A whole device-free session each month; device-free critique.
