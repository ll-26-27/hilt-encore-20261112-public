Require reflection videos to carry signs that they are unscripted (walking while talking, no reading from a screen), and teach that authenticity on camera is something you produce.

## How it runs

For each reflection, students walk, or do something with their hands, while they talk; no notes in view. In class, watch a few examples (with permission) and name the techniques that make speech sound unscripted. Then discuss: if authenticity can be produced, what is it evidence of?

## With Claude

Students ask Claude to transcribe a reflection and mark sentences that sound read or rehearsed versus improvised, then judge whether they agree.

## Why it works

It makes the weekly reflection a more reliable sign of the student's own thinking, and teaches a media lesson at the same time: every self-presentation is constructed.

## Time and materials

No extra time; a phone.

## Variations

Compare a scripted and an unscripted take of the same content; analyze how influencers produce "realness."
