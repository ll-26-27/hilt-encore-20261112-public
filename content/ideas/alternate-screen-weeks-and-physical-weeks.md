Plan the term so sessions alternate between work at screens and work with bodies, objects, and rooms, with AI in the background on the physical weeks.

## How it runs

Mark each session on the calendar as "screen" or "physical." On physical weeks, the activity uses cameras, materials, movement, or space, and any AI runs quietly to capture, caption, or display what people make.

## With Claude

For physical weeks, build with Claude Code a capture page that students use from a phone: they take a photo at a station, it uploads and appears on a live wall with an AI-written caption (ask for "a /capture page that uploads a photo with a station name and a /live page that shows the latest uploads in a grid, captioned by a vision model").

## Why it works

It keeps the course grounded in what people do in a room, and shows AI as a way to document and augment human work rather than replace it.

## Time and materials

Calendar planning; a projector; phones.

## Variations

Alternate within a session (screen hour, physical hour); one physical week per unit.
