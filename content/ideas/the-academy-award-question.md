Ask: would you rather win an Academy Award as the studio head of a film you had nothing to do with making, or make a film that good and not win?

## How it runs

Pose the question at the start of a course or unit. Students answer in one sentence and then talk in pairs about what it shows they value: the credit or the making. Return to it when a project is turned in.

## With Claude

None.

## Why it works

It surfaces the difference between producing an output and doing the work, which is the question AI raises for every assignment.

## Time and materials

Ten minutes.

## Variations

Substitute a prize in the course's field (a publication, a patent, a grade).
