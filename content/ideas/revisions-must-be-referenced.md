Let students revise anything before the final portfolio, as long as each revision says what changed and points to it.

## How it runs

State that all work can be revised and revision only helps, but every revised piece carries a short note: what changed, why, and where (a commit, a timestamp, a before/after). Original deadlines still apply to the first version.

## With Claude

In a repository, the note can be generated from the diff: "Summarize the changes between tag v1 and HEAD in plain language, and list the files changed." The student edits the summary into their own words.

## Why it works

It rewards iteration without hiding it, and makes revision itself something students can reflect on.

## Time and materials

No extra class time.

## Variations

A revision log as a graded portfolio section.
