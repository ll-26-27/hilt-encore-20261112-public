Change one variable in fixed steps through its whole range, capture each step, and lay the results out as a grid.

## How it runs

For lighting: one light moved to twelve positions around a subject's head, a photo at each "hour," head-on and in profile. Lay out the stills as a contact sheet and name each position. Students say which they like and why, in the vocabulary.

## With Claude

Ask Claude Code: "Make a contact sheet from the photos in `clock/`, labeled 1–12, and a page that shows them as a clock face." Optionally caption each with a vision model asked only to name the light's position.

## Why it works

A systematic series builds a vocabulary for what you like and don't, faster than random trying, and the grid makes cause and effect visible.

## Time and materials

20 minutes. A light, a camera, a subject.

## Variations

One parameter of a simulation; temperature in a chemistry demo; tempo in music; one word changed in a prompt.
