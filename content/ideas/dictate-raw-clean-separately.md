When thinking aloud with an AI, keep the raw dictation in one place and the cleaned-up, organized version in another, so your own words stay recoverable.

## How it runs

Record or dictate freely. Save the transcript untouched in a dated file. Then ask the AI to extract ideas into the working documents, clearly marked as cleaned or as its additions. When you need to quote yourself or check a decision, go back to the raw file.

## With Claude

Tell Claude Code: "Save what I dictate verbatim under '## dictation' in today's file. Then put cleaned-up versions, with your own ideas marked as yours, in the week folder." Keep the rule in `CLAUDE.md`.

## Why it works

It prevents the AI's polish from replacing the thinker's words, and keeps a provenance trail between what was said and what was planned.

## Time and materials

No extra time; a dictation app or voice mode.

## Variations

Students use the same rule for their project notes; a lab notebook with a raw and a clean page.
