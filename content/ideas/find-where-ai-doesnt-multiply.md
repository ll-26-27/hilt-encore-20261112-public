Have students try the same small build in a browser and with physical hardware, and notice where AI speeds them up and where it doesn't.

## How it runs

Pick a small goal (a light that responds to sound, a cue that fires on a word). Groups build it once as a web page with AI help and once with a microcontroller or other hardware. Compare time, frustration, and what they understood. Discuss where human knowledge is still the bottleneck.

## With Claude

Ask Claude Code to build the browser version end to end; for the hardware version, ask for wiring steps and code, then test on the device and report back what failed.

## Why it works

It replaces hype with a measured sense of where AI helps, and points to where human skill still matters most.

## Time and materials

One session; a microcontroller kit; laptops.

## Variations

Compare AI help on a data analysis with AI help on a lab bench procedure.
