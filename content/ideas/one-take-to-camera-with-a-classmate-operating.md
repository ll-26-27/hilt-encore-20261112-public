Each student talks for two or three minutes to a real camera, in one take, with a classmate running the camera and calling "rolling" and "cut."

## How it runs

Two minutes on paper first (bullet points, no script). Medium close-up. The operator says "rolling," the speaker talks about their toolbox (the tools and habits they work with), the operator says "cut." No second take: "Don't fix it; the recovery is more interesting than the plan." Clips are shared within the class only.

## With Claude

Transcribe the take (Claude Code can write a one-line transcription script) and keep the transcript as the first piece of the student's context document for the term.

## Why it works

Recording becomes normal on the first day, in a light frame, so later recorded reflections and interviews aren't the first time. Running the camera for someone else builds care for the speaker.

## Time and materials

5 minutes per student plus setup. A camera on a tripod, a light, a quiet corner.

## Variations

"Explain one idea from today's reading"; a 30-second elevator version; done in pairs on phones.
