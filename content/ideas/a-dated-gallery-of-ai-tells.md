Keep a shared gallery of the signs that something was made by AI, each with the date it was spotted and, later, the date it stopped being true.

## How it runs

Students submit examples of AI tells (six fingers, a certain phrasing, a lighting look, a stock code pattern) with the date and a short explanation. Review the gallery each month: retire tells that newer models fixed and note what replaced them.

## With Claude

Build the gallery with Claude Code as a folder of Markdown entries rendered as a page sorted by date (ask for "a page that reads entries/*.md with frontmatter title, date_spotted, date_retired, medium, and an image, and shows active and retired tells in two columns"). Students add entries by pull request.

## Why it works

Detection skills age quickly; dating them teaches students that the skill is noticing, not memorizing a list.

## Time and materials

Five minutes per entry; a shared repository.

## Variations

A gallery of writing tells only; a class "slop motif index."
