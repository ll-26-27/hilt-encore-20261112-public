Students draw how they currently take notes and turn them into papers or projects, then rebuild that workflow as a folder their AI works in.

## How it runs

On paper, each student diagrams their real process: where notes go, how sources are kept, how a draft gets made. Pairs compare. Then each student sets up a project folder that mirrors the diagram (sources, notes, drafts) and works in it with an AI for the rest of the session.

## With Claude

In Claude Code, ask: "Here is a photo of my workflow diagram. Create a folder structure that matches it, with a README in each folder explaining what goes there, and a CLAUDE.md that tells you how I want you to use these folders." What gets built: the folder tree and the instruction file.

## Why it works

Starting from the student's own habits makes the AI workflow theirs, and making the process explicit is a lesson in metacognition.

## Time and materials

Forty-five minutes; paper; Claude Code.

## Variations

Draw a research lab's workflow, a newsroom's, a studio's; redraw at the end of term.
