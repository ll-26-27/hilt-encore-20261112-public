Let students use AI all the way through producing a short video, and put the grade in a long oral exam about it, where they must choose the right connections to make, not list them all.

## How it runs

Students make a three-minute video on a course topic with any AI help they like. Each then has a twenty- to thirty-minute oral exam: they present, and the examiner asks them to connect the video to other course material. They are graded on choosing and defending the right connection.

## With Claude

Students prepare by asking Claude to quiz them on connections between their video and the syllabus (with the syllabus and readings loaded); the examiner can use a question skill on the student's files (see "A question skill that reads the work").

## Why it works

The artifact shows what they could make; the oral exam shows what they know. Knowing many connections is the representation; choosing one and saying it well is the performance.

## Time and materials

Twenty-five minutes per student; a recording setup.

## Variations

A poster or a website in place of the video; a group version.
