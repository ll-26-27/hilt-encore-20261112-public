Divide a craft process into stages, give each stage to a different group in timed rotations, and discuss what happens at the hand-offs.

## How it runs

Pick a process that takes an hour and has distinct stages (block printing: trace and transfer, cut, ink and print). Three groups rotate every 20 minutes, each continuing the last group's work. At the end, compare results and discuss what was lost, kept, or invented at each hand-off. Then ask: where in an AI-assisted workflow are the hand-offs, and who checks them?

## With Claude

Run a second relay with agents: in Claude Code, have three subagents each do one stage of a writing or coding task in turn, each told only to continue the last one's file, and compare their hand-off notes with the human groups'.

## Why it works

It makes division of labor and its hand-off problems tangible; a film's credits list hundreds of people, and a one-sentence prompt collapses all of that, so re-dividing the work is one way back to intention and skill.

## Time and materials

60 minutes. Craft materials; optionally Claude Code.

## Variations

A relay essay (outline, draft, revise); a relay lab report; an exquisite corpse.
