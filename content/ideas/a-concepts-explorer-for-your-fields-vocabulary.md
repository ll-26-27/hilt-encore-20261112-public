Build a browsable reference of the key concepts of practice in several fields, and a view that shows the same word as each field defines it.

## How it runs

Students pick a word that recurs across disciplines (study, rehearsal, iteration, imitation, feedback). They read how each field uses it, side by side, and write the version that fits the thing they're making this week.

## With Claude

Ask Claude to write one Markdown file per concept per field (title, one-line description, an entry of two or three paragraphs with a concrete example and links to related entries), checked for accuracy, then ask Claude Code for a page that lists categories, renders entries, searches titles and descriptions, and shows any concept that appears in several fields side by side.

## Why it works

Students see that the unit of practice has a name in every discipline, and that what they're doing (a study, a sketch, a drill) is a recognized move with a craft behind it.

## Time and materials

An afternoon to generate and review a few hundred entries; an hour to build the explorer.

## Variations

A glossary for one course's technical terms; a cross-cultural vocabulary (one concept across languages); a methods vocabulary across sciences.
