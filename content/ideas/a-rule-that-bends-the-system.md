Give an agent system one arbitrary rule and watch how it changes the agents' behavior.

## How it runs

Run a word game where a judge may not accept before the third try. Read the log: what do the players do when a correct answer is rejected? Change the rule (minimum one try, minimum five) and rerun. Discuss what the rule taught the agents.

## With Claude

Any agent theater or Claude Code script with a judge and a minimum-tries rule in the judge's prompt.

## Why it works

In one recorded run, the guesser said the right word on try one, was told it was wrong, avoided that word for five more tries, and only came back to it on the seventh. Students see how a system's rules, not the model's intelligence, shape outcomes, a lesson that transfers to grading rubrics and institutions.

## Time and materials

20 minutes; a cent per run.

## Variations

Rubrics in peer review; turn-taking rules in a debate; incentive design in economics.
