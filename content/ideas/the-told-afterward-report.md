After each class, the instructor records a few minutes of what happened, and an AI turns it into a dated report beside the plan, so plan and outcome can be compared.

## How it runs

Within a day of each session, talk for three to five minutes: what ran, what changed from the plan, what surprised you, what to do next time. File the recording's transcript in that session's folder under "what happened." At the end of a unit, have the AI draft a story of the unit from the plans and the reports, labeling which is which.

## With Claude

In Claude Code: "Add this transcript to weeks/04/README.md under 'What happened', dated, verbatim. Then list three differences between the plan above and what happened." At term's end: "Write a one-page account of each week from its plan and its 'what happened' section; quote my words only from the transcripts and mark everything else as plan or your summary."

## Why it works

Teaching knowledge is lost when it isn't written down the same day; keeping plan and outcome side by side makes the next iteration and any reporting honest.

## Time and materials

Five minutes after each class.

## Variations

Co-teachers alternate; students record a thirty-second "what happened" too.
