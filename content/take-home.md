## TDM 155AI: Making Stuff in the Age of AI

Our course this term, one public repository per week. Each is a working app the class runs on its own machines; clone one, open it with Claude Code, and change it.

- [All the course's public repositories](https://github.com/tdm155ai)
- [Week 1: the image experiment](https://tdm155ai.github.io/week-1-image-experiment/). Six prompts across twelve image models, 72 images, compared side by side.
- [Week 2: code and setup](https://github.com/tdm155ai/tdm155ai-week-2). The setup tutorial, from a GitHub account to Git, the command line, and the Claude and ChatGPT desktop apps, plus scripts that call image and text models.
- [Week 3: tools of the AV trade](https://github.com/tdm155ai/tdm155ai-week-3). Cameras, lenses, light, and sound: a station checklist, a glossary, and a live wall of what the room captures.
- [Week 6: studies and skills](https://github.com/tdm155ai/tdm155ai-week-6). Concepts, Claude skills, and 49 illustrated cases of makers' sequences, studies, and practice.
- [Week 7: editing](https://github.com/tdm155ai/tdm155ai-week-7). One sheet per editing app and command-line tool (ffmpeg, ImageMagick, SoX), every example run for real.
- [Week 8: the interview machine](https://github.com/tdm155ai/tdm155ai-week-8). Podcast-recording software built for the class: it records two or more microphones, cuts and transcribes each speaker as they talk, and offers an AI producer on request. A copy is there for students to remix.
- [Week 9: agents, systems, orchestration](https://github.com/tdm155ai/tdm155ai-week-9). Systems of agents to run and remix, and a podcast-prep ensemble.
- [Week 9: a sample context folder](https://github.com/tdm155ai/tdm155ai-week-9/tree/main/sample-context-repo). An invented student's whole project as a folder an AI can read: sources, notes, process logs, and what not to read. This is the shape of the context folders students share with each other.
- [Week 10: installations](https://github.com/tdm155ai/tdm155ai-week-10). Launching sound, video, and light for a live audience with keys, MIDI, voice cues, and hand tracking.

## Workshops for faculty

- [Claude workshop materials](https://bok-learning-lab.github.io/claude-workshop-materials/). Our faculty workshop on Claude: getting started, an encyclopedia of terms and cheat sheets, and the activities.
- [The recipe-card activity](https://bok-learning-lab.github.io/claude-workshop-materials/activities/recipes/). Photos of old recipe cards in, a web gallery out: Claude reads the cards, renames the files, researches each recipe, and builds the site.
- [Getting started with Claude](https://bok-learning-lab.github.io/claude-workshop-materials/encyclopedia/get-started/). Setup guides for the Harvard account, Cowork, and Claude Code on Mac and Windows.
- [Desktop AI app workshop](https://github.com/ll-26-27/desktop-app-workshop). Working with an AI assistant in a folder of files, in ChatGPT Desktop or Claude Desktop: one walkthrough, six further use cases, and a blank project for your own.
- [Department workshop pages](https://ll-26-27.github.io/dept-workshop-pages/). Interactive pages from a department workshop on AI in teaching.

## Tools and starters

- [Who is speaking? Where the chatbot came from](https://eng298ai-20261020.vercel.app/). Talk to ELIZA, run GPT-2 with and without the first assistant script, and build a bot in ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude.
- [Claude Code and vibe coding for the humanities](https://github.com/ll-26-27/eng298ai-20261103). A seminar's introduction: describe what you want, have Claude build it with you, then inspect, test, and revise.
- [The same Pokédex, two ways](https://github.com/ll-26-27/socstd98to-sqlite-pokedex-public). A 20-minute activity: query a database by hand in SQL, then ask Claude to build a front end on the same data.
- [AI tools for journalists](https://github.com/ll-26-27/ai-tools-for-journalists). A capture station: point a camera at a document, notes, or a scene, and get it transcribed or described.
- [A basic Slack agent](https://github.com/ll-26-27/basic-slack-agent-27). A starter Slack app with a step-by-step terminal walkthrough.
- [Spectrogram](https://github.com/ll-26-27/spectrogram-next). The Chrome Music Lab spectrogram, ported to Next.js.
- [Learning Lab origin stories](https://bok-learning-lab.github.io/ll-origin-stories/). Eight-panel comics from our origin-story workshops, with the skill that makes them.
