## What this is

A browser drawing study after Vera Molnar's *Interruptions*: short lines on a grid, turned by chance, with areas left empty. Change the density, angle, line length, and size of the gaps; the plotter redraws the composition. Keep the rules and press **New chance** for another drawing, or **Save** the result.

## How we made it

Built for the Learning Lab's installation studies in November 2026. A seeded random-number generator makes the choices repeatable, and a canvas draws the lines in sequence. The interactive below comes from the original CV installation project. It runs in the browser without a model call.
