## What this is

A scrollytelling film analysis built for **GENED 1049: East Asian Cinema** in spring 2026. Seven clips from Zhang Yimou’s *To Live* (1994) sit beside an analysis of shot size, lighting, and editing. Scroll to advance the film; pause to look closely at a frame. The page makes the connection between an observation and the shot it describes visible as you read.

## How we made it

The original Next.js app divides a dialogue sequence into seven video clips prepared for smooth seeking. Each clip stays in view while the reader scrolls through its section. Scroll position sets the video’s playback position, and an overlay presents the shot type, a short interpretation, and closer reading notes. The sequence moves from an establishing shot through tighter framings and a detail of an exchange, then returns to the wider space.

This showcase embeds the original deployed story unchanged. Its clips and commentary load from the GENED 1049 site; no model call or API key is needed. The repository’s latest recorded update is April 16, 2026. The index card is a frame from the story’s fifth clip; the example page leads directly into the interactive story.
