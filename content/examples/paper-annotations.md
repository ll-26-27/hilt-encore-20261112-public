## What this is

A gallery of annotation activities from the Learning Lab: people working around a shared map, photographed syllabus annotations, and the resulting ANE 140 database. Watch the clips and open the database image at full size to follow the path from marks on paper to structured records.

The activity keeps the material work in the room. Students draw, circle, connect, and write; the class can then examine patterns in its own observations.

## How we made it

The activity's map, timeline, or syllabus provides the structure for a database. Photographs of the annotated sheets are read with a vision model, and the extracted records retain links to their source images and the material being annotated. The screenshot shows fields for annotation type, text, location, bounding box, and ink color.

The source account describes deployments across 2025–26, including NELC maps and timelines and ANE 140 and HISTLIT 90FK syllabus activities, following an earlier COMPLIT 207 prototype. This gallery uses the four GIFs and database screenshot supplied in that account. All five media files are hosted with this site.
