## What this is

Why do onions take so long to caramelize? How does a basil sphere’s shell grow? These GENED 1104 examples turn kitchen observations into equations, controls and things that move. Choose a simulation below and try changing one variable at a time.

## How we made it

The work starts before the code: identify what changes, give it a unit, choose a physical relationship, and decide what that relationship should look like. Then an assistant turns the model into a single HTML page. We check its numbers and its limits against the kitchen question. The four pages here are the saved course examples, with their original models intact.
