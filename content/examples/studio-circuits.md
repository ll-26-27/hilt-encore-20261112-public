## What this is

A workbench for three physical-computing studies: one LED, eight addressable pixels, and a triangular light sculpture. Orbit the model, follow five connections, then start the light preview. Change its rhythm, brightness, and color; the Arduino sketch changes with it.

## How we made it

The November 2026 installation project began with photographs of the materials on the studio table. Those photographs sit alongside a Three.js model, connection tables, step-by-step lessons, and downloadable sketches. The model offers a way to rehearse a build and see how a small circuit becomes a spatial idea.

The browser preview is a visual study. It does not connect to hardware or test a physical circuit; the original lessons name the reference components and assumptions for each build.
