## What this is

Choose a concept, a case study, and a theory, then practice connecting them in an oral argument. **Elisa, a Learning Lab Undergraduate Fellow (LLUF), demonstrates the activity** below. Follow her cards, notes, and full presentation without a microphone or API key.

## How we made it

The fall 2026 practice app adapted an earlier oral-practice scaffold around the course's own decks and illustrated cards. Its original flow deals three options per deck, offers a five-minute preparation window, records a four-to-six-minute presentation, transcribes it, and returns short, ungraded practice feedback.

This version works without recording or model calls. The draw is reconstructed around Elisa's actual choices, with two additional options per deck to illustrate the selection step. The preparation outline is reconstructed from her transcript. Her full wording is preserved with paragraph breaks for readability. The final feedback is newly written for this showcase, follows the app's three-part format, and is clearly distinguished from her presentation. It is not a saved response from her session.
