## What this is

Six plain prompts ("an artist," "a performer," "portrait of an artist," "main character of a story," and two more) sent once each to twelve image models: 72 images. Group them by prompt or by model, filter, enlarge, download. It was the first week of our course, and the point was to look: what does each model assume an artist looks like, and how differently do they assume it? Every request and response is saved beside the images, so it's a record, not a mood board.

## How we made it

We asked for a repeat of a comparison from the day before, with exact prompts and no embellishment, through Harvard's HUIT AI gateways and OpenRouter. Prompts were added as we went, from three to six, and then we asked for it to be published as a gallery. The generation took about two and a half hours on the morning of September 9; OpenRouter charged 81 cents for its eighteen images. What went wrong: an image-size setting HUIT had rejected the day before had to be left out, and the browser check of the gallery's controls stalled. And a caveat we kept on the page: twelve model IDs include aliases and a dated snapshot, so they aren't necessarily twelve different models. It's an exploration, not a benchmark.
